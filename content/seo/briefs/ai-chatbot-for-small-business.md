## Search intent

Learn. A small business owner has heard chatbots can answer customers for them and wants to know what one really does, whether it's worth it for a business their size, how it would know their prices and services, what it costs and what can go wrong. They expect a plain definition first, then an honest "is this for me".

## What they miss

- None of them says what the visitor sees when the AI provider is down or a free quota runs out. "Add fallback responses" is as far as they go.
- Nobody explains in plain words how the bot finds the right page before it answers, so "grounded in your content" is a promise the reader can't check.
- Vendors don't publish a measured free-tier limit, so "start for free" has no number attached.
- Most results are written by companies selling a subscription, so the custom-build option is missing.
- Almost no result gives the owner a test they can run on a bot before paying for it.

## Our angle

A guide built around a chatbot that is running on the page you're reading: how it finds answers, what it cost to run on a free tier, what visitors see when the AI fails, and a test any owner can run on a bot before paying for one.

## Unique additions

- Measured free-tier numbers from a live bot (2026-10-26): 20 replies per day, first token in 1–2 s, Flash-Lite models at 13–40 s per reply.
- The three-layer failure plan (AI reply, saved answers, handoff to a person), which readers can test on this site.
- A pre-launch test checklist an owner can run on any bot.
- A decision table for no bot, subscription or custom build, using real build prices.
