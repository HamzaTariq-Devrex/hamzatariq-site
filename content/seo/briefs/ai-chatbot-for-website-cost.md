## What they miss

- Almost every result is written by a company selling a chatbot subscription, so the "build" option is either ignored or priced like an enterprise project ($8,000+), which is not what a small business site needs.
- None of them say what happens when the AI provider fails or a free quota runs out. That is the hidden running cost: a bot that shows an error loses the lead.
- Free tiers are mentioned as "free" without limits. Nobody measured how many replies a free tier allows or how fast it answers.
- Per-resolution and overage fees are listed but not tied to a decision: which billing model suits a site with few chats.
- No result gives the questions a buyer should ask a seller to find the real total cost.
