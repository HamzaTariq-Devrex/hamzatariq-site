## Search intent

Compare. A founder or small business owner choosing between paying monthly for a ready-made tool (SaaS) and having a custom dashboard built. They want the verdict first, a side-by-side table, and a way to decide for their own business with real prices, not "it depends".

## Top results

1. [Geckoboard: Should you build or buy dashboards?](https://www.geckoboard.com/blog/dashboard-build-vs-buy/): buy for internal KPI dashboards, build for customer-facing analytics; comparison table; vendor pitch for its own product.
2. [Zoho Analytics: Build vs Buy a Dashboard Tool](https://www.zoho.com/analytics/insightshq/build-vs-buy-dashboard-tool.html): three meanings of "building a dashboard", cost model for a mid-size team with internal inconsistencies, five questions; vendor pitch.

5. [Luzmo: Why custom software development isn't worth the cost](https://luzmo.com/blog/custom-software-development-vs-off-the-shelf-software): for SaaS product teams, argues for off-the-shelf components; vendor pitch.

## What they miss

- Almost all treat "dashboard" as analytics charts (KPI or BI). None covers the operations dashboard a small business actually runs on: orders, stock, customers, staff updating records.
- None gives a checklist for leaving a ready-made tool: data export, who can see what, price per extra user, what happens to your data if you cancel.
- The hybrid (keep the tool for what it does well, add a small custom screen) is mentioned only as a vendor upsell.
- None gives a real fixed starting price from someone who builds them; the cost numbers are vendor estimates for mid-size teams.
