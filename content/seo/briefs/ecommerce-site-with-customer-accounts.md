## Search intent

Buy. A store owner planning a new online store (or outgrowing one) wants to know whether to pay for customer logins, what an account area includes, and how much more it costs than a store with guest checkout. They expect a price first, then what they get for it and whether they need it.

## Top results

1. [Customer accounts vs. guest checkout: how to use each (BigCommerce)](https://bigcommerce.com/glossary/customer-accounts-vs-guest-checkout-how-use-each): short glossary piece; offer both and prompt for an account after checkout. Lists only saved billing info and saved items as account features; no prices, no privacy.
2. [Guest Checkout: Benefits, Best Practices, and How-To (Shopify)](https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/guest-checkout): the most thorough; trade-offs and best practices, cites Baymard on shoppers abandoning when forced to create an account; promotes Shopify's own tools. No custom-build cost, little on what the account area contains.
3. [Save Account Creation for the Confirmation Step (Baymard)](https://baymard.com/blog/delayed-account-creation): research piece arguing to offer an account after the order is placed and to list the account's benefits on the confirmation page.
4. [Guest Checkout vs. Customer Accounts (Miva)](https://blog.miva.com/guest-checkout-vs.-customer-accounts): short pros and cons, a note on B2B and repeat buyers; vendor demo CTA; from 2022.

## What they miss

- None gives a price for building a custom store with accounts, or the difference between a store without them and one with them.
- None lists everything an account area really includes (order history, order status, saved addresses, reorder, password reset or email sign-in links, account deletion) or the admin side that comes with it.
- None mentions what storing customer accounts commits the owner to: passwords, personal data, deletion requests, security updates.
- All are written by platform vendors (or a research firm selling audits); none says when a store should not pay for accounts.

## Our angle

Accounts are a $1,000 step up from my store package ($1,000 → $2,000), so they're worth it only when customers come back and need something only an account can give them: order history, reorders, saved addresses or member prices. Guest checkout stays either way.
