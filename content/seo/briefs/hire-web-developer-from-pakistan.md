## Search intent

Hire. A founder or small business owner in the US, UK, EU or Middle East who is considering a developer based in Pakistan, usually to save money. They want to know what to check, whether it's a sensible idea, how payment and working hours work, what it costs and what to watch out for. The results are mostly marketplace directories (Twine, Contra, Upwork, Freelancer), so the searcher is also deciding between a marketplace and hiring someone directly.

## Top results

1. [AAMAX: Web developer in Pakistan](https://aamax.co/blog/web-developer-in-pakistan): agency overview (talent, skills, hiring models, evaluation, pricing, risks). Unsourced hourly rate bands and graduate counts. Doesn't name payment methods or explain how to confirm that portfolio work is the developer's own.
2. [Contra: web developers in Pakistan](https://contra.com/hire/web-developers-in-pakistan): marketplace directory with a long FAQ block; unsourced salary and rate figures and "cost reduction" claims; contradicts other sources on PayPal. No red flags, no written terms.
3. [nsave: PayPal alternatives in Pakistan](https://nsave.com/pakistan/paypal-alternative): payment provider's article on how people in Pakistan receive money from abroad (Payoneer, direct bank transfer and others). Vendor page; fees unsourced.
4. [Twine: web developers in Pakistan](https://www.twine.net/find/web-developers/pk): directory of profiles with a first-hire discount; no guidance at all.

## What they miss

- Nothing written by a developer in Pakistan about how the work actually runs from this side (payment, overlap, written terms).
- None explains how to check that a portfolio site was really built by that person rather than by the agency they worked at.
- None gives a red-flag list specific to hiring directly from Pakistan, or a safe way to start (small first project, staged payments).
- Rates are unsourced ranges; none gives a real, fixed price list with delivery times and terms.
- Sources disagree about payment methods; none simply tells the buyer to agree the method in writing up front.
