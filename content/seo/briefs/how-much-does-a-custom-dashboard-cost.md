## Search intent

Buy. A founder or business owner who runs orders, stock, customers or bookings from spreadsheets wants a number they can budget with, what drives it up, and what they get for it. They expect a price (or a range) first, then the factors, then how to get an accurate quote.

## Top results

1. [DhiWise: Admin panel development cost](https://www.dhiwise.com/post/admin-panel-development-cost): hourly rates by role, a range from "basic" to enterprise, hosting and maintenance as a yearly percentage, then a pitch for its own AI app builder. No sources, no real projects, no fixed prices.
2. [Cloudswitched: Custom reporting and dashboard development cost in the UK](https://www.cloudswitched.com/blog/custom-reporting-dashboard-cost-uk-2026): long UK guide on BI dashboards (Power BI, Tableau, custom), per-feature price bands, real-time premiums and maintenance. Unsourced market stats, internal arithmetic that doesn't add up, aimed at mid-size companies.
3. [Cadence: Cost to build an admin dashboard](https://cadence.withremote.ai/blog/cost-to-build-an-admin-dashboard): the most structured result; separates an internal CRUD viewer, an ops console and a customer-facing surface, compares hiring routes and low-code tools. Vendor pitch for weekly engineer booking, no payment terms.
4. [Kolonell: Custom management dashboard pricing](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/custom-management-dashboard-pricing-2026): BI tool vs custom app setup and licence costs in EUR, one unverifiable case study, headline range that doesn't match its own table.

## What they miss

- None gives a real, fixed starting price from someone who builds these; every result gives ranges so wide (thousands to six figures) that a small business can't plan with them.
- They mix up three different products: a BI reporting dashboard, an admin dashboard for running daily operations, and a full web app with its own users. The price difference between them is the main thing a buyer needs to know.
- No payment terms, revision rounds or support period, so the reader can't compare quotes like for like.
- Ongoing cost is either skipped or given as an unsourced percentage.
- Nothing on what the buyer should prepare to get an accurate quote quickly.
