## What they miss

- Most are written by agencies selling one side, and the verdict follows the seller. Few say plainly when WordPress is the better choice.
- Nobody shows measured numbers from a real site before and after; speed and SEO claims are general.
- Several imply Next.js ranks better by itself. One skeptical result points out that hosting quality and execution matter more than the framework; most top results skip that.
- Cost comparisons give yearly estimates without a real price list, and ignore the costs that come after launch (plugin licences, fixes after updates) or count them without saying where they come from.
- Moving an existing WordPress site without losing rankings is rarely covered, though it's the first worry of an owner who already ranks.
