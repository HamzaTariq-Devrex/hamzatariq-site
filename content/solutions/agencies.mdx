## Why agencies use white-label web development

Agencies win work faster than they can hire. White-label web development lets you say yes to more projects without adding full-time developers: you keep the client relationship and the margin, and the build happens behind the scenes under your brand.

The risk is reliability. A developer who misses deadlines or stops replying makes your agency look bad. So the things that matter most are clear quotes, regular previews and a developer who works in hours that overlap with yours.

## How white-label web development for agencies works with me

You send the brief or the designs. I reply with a written quote based on my published packages, so you can add your margin and quote your client with confidence. Payment is 50% upfront and 50% on delivery for each project.

While I build, you get staging links you can show your client as your own work, and I work in your repository and tools if you prefer. Every project includes 2 free revision rounds, and I'm available in US, UK and EU time zones for calls.

After launch, the code, hosting and accounts are handed over in your or your client's name, with 30 days of free bug fixes.
