## What patients look for before they book

Choosing a dentist or a clinic is personal. Before anyone books, they want to know three things: do you offer the treatment they need, will they be treated kindly, and how easy is it to get an appointment.

A good website for clinics and dental practices answers those questions on the first screen. Treatments are explained in plain words, with what happens during the visit. Your team has faces and names. And booking is one clear button, not a phone number that only works during office hours.

## How I'd plan a website for clinics and dental practices

I'd start with your most important treatments, the ones that bring in new patients, and give each one its own page. Each page explains the treatment, who it's for and what to expect, which helps nervous patients and gives Google a clear page for each search.

Next is the booking flow. If you already use a booking system, the site links to it. If not, patients send a request and your front desk confirms it from a simple [dashboard](/services/dashboards).

Then the routine questions. An [AI assistant](/services/ai-chatbots) can answer opening hours, parking, preparation and which treatments you offer, using only your own pages. It's set up to refuse medical questions and point patients to your team instead.
