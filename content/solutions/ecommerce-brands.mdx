## Why brands outgrow theme stores

Themes are a good way to start selling. They become a problem when the brand grows up. The product photos get better, the prices go up, and suddenly the store looks like a template next to the products it sells.

Speed is the other problem. Every app added to a theme store loads its own scripts on every page. On a phone, that's the difference between a shopper seeing the product and a shopper going back to Instagram.

A custom ecommerce website for brands fixes both. The design starts from your products and photography, and only the code a page needs is sent to the browser.

## How I'd plan your store

I start with how your customers actually buy. Do they browse collections, search for one product, or arrive from an Instagram post straight to a product page? The answer decides what the home page, collection pages and product pages need to do.

Then I look at how your team works after the order comes in. The [admin dashboard](/services/dashboards) is built around that: the orders to pack today, products running low, and customers waiting for a reply.
