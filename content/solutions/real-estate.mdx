## Why real estate website development is different

A property website is really a search tool. Buyers arrive with a budget and an area in mind, and they judge your agency by how fast they can find matching homes. If the filters are slow or the photos take ages to load, they go back to the big portals.

The second half is what happens after the enquiry. A lead that waits a day for a reply is often gone. So real estate website development isn't only about the public site; it's about getting every enquiry to the right agent quickly.

## How I'd plan your site

I'd start with your listings data: what details every property has, how often listings change, and where they come from today. That decides whether listings are managed in a custom [dashboard](/services/dashboards) or pulled from a system you already use.

Then the search experience. Filters for area, price, type and size have to respond instantly, and each property page needs big photos, the key facts at the top and a clear enquiry button.

Finally, the lead flow. Every enquiry lands in one list with the property it's about, gets assigned to an agent and moves through statuses until it's closed. An [AI assistant](/services/ai-chatbots) can handle the first questions at night and collect the buyer's budget and timeline before an agent picks it up.
