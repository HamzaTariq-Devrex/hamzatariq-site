## What SaaS marketing website development has to cover

A SaaS marketing site has one job: get the right visitor to try the product. That means explaining in a few seconds what the product does and who it's for, answering the questions that block a sign-up, and showing up in search for the problems the product solves.

Most early-stage sites fail on the second and third points. The home page is clear enough, but pricing questions go unanswered, and the pages were built inside a client-side React app that search engines barely read.

## How I'd plan your site

I'd start with your best customers: what problem they had, what they searched for, and what convinced them to sign up. Those answers shape the home page message, the feature pages and the first articles for the blog.

The site itself is a separate [Next.js](/services/nextjs-websites) project, pre-rendered for speed, with every page's title, description and social preview set in code. Your team can add landing pages for campaigns without touching the app.

If your marketing pages already exist as a React single-page app, the fastest win is often a [React to Next.js migration](/services/react-nextjs-seo): same components and design, but real HTML for every URL.
